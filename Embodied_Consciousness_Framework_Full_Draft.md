# From Self-Stabilizing Dynamics to Conscious Experience
## A Physical and Ontological Framework for Embodied Consciousness

**Author:** Jorge Simão

---

# Abstract

This paper proposes a unified research programme connecting embodied cognition, dynamical systems, theoretical neuroscience, physics, and philosophy of mind. Rather than treating cognition and consciousness as separate problems, the framework argues that both emerge from the same class of self-stabilizing physical organizations.

The proposal rests on three complementary ideas. First, cognition is understood as the progressive stabilization of perception-action loops, giving rise to hierarchical representations of increasingly invariant regularities in the world. Second, these processes are most naturally described by multiscale activation-correlation structures rather than isolated neuron activity. Third, because neural computation is implemented through moving electrical charge, these organized dynamics necessarily generate coherent electromagnetic field organization. Conscious experience is proposed to correspond not to the electromagnetic field itself, but to the ontological realization of this metastable physical organization.

The paper further argues that the traditional hard problem of consciousness can be reformulated. Rather than asking why neural activity generates subjective experience, the question of qualia becomes part of the broader ontological question of why organized physical existence possesses an intrinsic aspect at all. This does not eliminate the mystery of existence, but removes consciousness as a unique anomaly within science.

---

# 1. Introduction

The study of consciousness has historically followed two largely independent traditions. Neuroscience has sought mechanistic explanations of perception, memory, attention, and action, while philosophy has focused on the subjective character of experience. This separation has produced the familiar distinction between the "easy problems" of cognition and the "hard problem" of consciousness.

This paper argues that this separation may itself be misleading. A unified framework may emerge if cognition is understood as the formation of self-stabilizing physical organizations that continuously couple organism and environment.

The purpose of this paper is not to claim a final solution, but to present a coherent research programme with explicit conceptual foundations and experimentally testable predictions.

# 2. Mechanistic Theory of Embodied Cognition

## 2.1 Perception as Dynamic Stabilization

Perception is proposed to be an active process of stabilization rather than passive encoding. Organism and environment continuously interact until mutually compatible sensorimotor states are achieved.

## 2.2 Action Completes Perception

Action changes future sensory input. Stable perception therefore requires closed perception-action loops.

## 2.3 Hierarchical Meta-Stability

Lower neural circuits stabilize rapidly while higher cortical systems stabilize over longer spatial and temporal scales. Increasingly invariant concepts emerge naturally through repeated stabilization.

Examples include edges, objects, places, affordances, social situations, self-models, and abstract concepts.

## 2.4 Multiscale Invariants

The same stabilization principle applies over milliseconds, seconds, learning, development, and evolution.

# 3. Activation-Correlation Structures

Traditional neuroscience often emphasizes firing rates or localized activity. This framework instead proposes that the relevant computational object is the activation-correlation structure (ACS): the evolving pattern of temporal correlations, synchrony, recurrence, and causal interaction across neural populations.

ACS provides a natural bridge between microscopic neural activity and macroscopic cognition.

Predictions include:

- Stable percepts correspond to stable ACS.
- Conscious transitions correspond to ACS reorganization.
- Dreaming, anesthesia, and disorders of consciousness should exhibit characteristic ACS signatures.

---

# 4. Electromagnetic Organization

Neural computation is implemented by ionic currents. Moving charge necessarily generates electromagnetic fields.

The brain therefore cannot avoid producing complex EM fields.

Most microscopic electrical activity contributes incoherent background fluctuations produced by thermal motion, metabolism, glia, chemical reactions, and dense cellular organization.

However, recurrent self-sustaining neural loops can generate coherent activation-correlation structures with significantly higher signal-to-noise ratios than the surrounding background.

The proposal is not that EM fields independently generate consciousness. Rather, coherent fields represent a coarse-grained physical manifestation of the underlying metastable neural organization.

---

# 5. Consciousness as Ontological Realization

The central hypothesis is that conscious experience is identical with the existence of a particular class of organized physical reality.

No inner observer is required.

No additional mental substance is introduced.

Instead, the internally stabilized physical world constructed through embodied interaction simply exists.

Its existence is the subjective perspective.

---

# 6. Qualia and Language Games

Discussions of qualia often assume sharp categories: conscious versus unconscious, red versus non-red, experience versus non-experience.

Following insights inspired by language-game analyses, these distinctions may partly reflect linguistic conventions rather than fundamental discontinuities in nature.

Experience may instead occupy a continuous spectrum of organizational richness.

Simple systems may possess minimal internal organization.

Complex organisms instantiate increasingly rich internally stabilized worlds.

This view dissolves many binary debates into quantitative questions.

---

# 7. Mapping the Hard Problem onto Ontology

Traditional formulation:

Why do physical systems produce experience?

Proposed reformulation:

Why does organized physical existence possess an intrinsic aspect?

Under this view, asking why qualia exists becomes analogous to asking why spacetime exists, why quantum fields exist, or why there is something rather than nothing.

The explanatory burden shifts from neuroscience alone to ontology itself.

Progress in understanding the ontology of physical existence therefore simultaneously becomes progress toward understanding consciousness.

---

# 8. Particle Analogy

Modern physics frequently interprets particles as metastable excitations of underlying quantum fields.

By analogy, conscious episodes may be interpreted as metastable organizations within neural physical dynamics.

The analogy is conceptual rather than quantum-mechanical.

The emphasis is on emergence, stability, and organization.

---

# 9. Internal Physical Worlds

External reality constrains the organism.

Through continual interaction, the nervous system constructs a dynamically maintained internal physical world.

This internal world:

- is physically instantiated,
- is causally effective,
- mirrors multiscale invariants of the external world,
- remains partially autonomous through recurrent dynamics.

Conscious experience corresponds to the existence of this internal world.

# 10. Experimental Programme

Potential empirical directions include:

* Mapping activation-correlation structures during stable perception.
* Comparing correlation signatures across modalities.
* Identifying minimal ACS associated with conscious report.
* Characterizing transitions during sleep, anesthesia, meditation, and psychedelics.
* Brain lesion studies examining degradation of ACS.
* Neural organoids and cultured networks.
* Artificial embodied agents implementing self-stabilizing loops.

A central prediction is that consciousness should correlate more strongly with multiscale activation-correlation organization than with simple firing rates or anatomical localization.

# 12. Discussion

Strengths:

- Compatible with mainstream neuroscience.
- Compatible with physicalism.
- Avoids dualism.
- Avoids strong panpsychism.
- Generates experimental predictions.
- Naturally links cognition and consciousness.

Limitations:

- Does not explain why existence itself exists.
- Does not claim EM fields alone are sufficient.
- Requires future computational and experimental validation.

# 13. Conclusion

The proposed framework views cognition and consciousness as different descriptions of the same underlying physical process.

Self-stabilizing perception-action loops generate hierarchical metastable representations of the world. These are expressed physically through multiscale activation-correlation structures and coherent electromagnetic organization.

Conscious experience is identified not as an additional substance, but as the intrinsic existence of an internally instantiated physical world maintained through these self-sustaining dynamics.

Rather than treating qualia as a unique anomaly, the framework relocates the remaining mystery to ontology itself: the nature of existence. In doing so, it provides a unified research programme spanning neuroscience, cognitive science, physics, artificial intelligence, and philosophy.


# 14. Mathematical Perspective

Although the framework is primarily conceptual, it naturally admits mathematical formalization.

Possible state variables include:

- Neural state vector x(t)
- Activation-correlation matrix C(t)
- Hierarchical metastable manifolds M_i
- Embodied perception-action graph G
- Electromagnetic field E(x,t)

Future work may characterize cognition as trajectories through a hierarchy of metastable attractors whose stability is maintained through continual interaction with the environment.

# 15. Relation to Artificial Intelligence

The framework suggests that current deep learning systems primarily learn statistical mappings rather than maintaining persistent embodied perception-action loops.

Future AI systems could instead be constructed around:

- continual interaction,
- self-maintained internal states,
- multiscale stabilization,
- hierarchical abstraction through invariants,
- activation-correlation dynamics rather than purely feed-forward representations.

This does not imply such systems are conscious, but provides an experimentally grounded research direction.

# 16. Falsifiable Predictions

The framework makes several testable predictions.

1. Activation-correlation structures should predict conscious report better than firing rates alone.

2. Similar conscious contents across individuals should exhibit convergent multiscale correlation organization despite individual neural variability.

3. Progressive degradation of correlation organization should correlate with progressive loss of phenomenological richness.

4. Artificial embodied systems implementing comparable self-stabilizing dynamics should reproduce many cognitive signatures before human-level intelligence.

5. Coherent large-scale EM organization should closely track metastable activation-correlation structures, without requiring an independent causal role.
