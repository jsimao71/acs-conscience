## Main conceptual changes

1. Separated mechanistic proposal, empirical hypothesis, and ontological identity proposal throughout.
2. Recast cognition as active stabilization under environmental, bodily, action-generated, and endogenous perturbation.
3. Added self-perturbation, metastability, and dynamic homeostasis, including bounded variability, local/global regulation, and explicit failure regimes.
4. Developed abstraction as invariant pickup across increasing perturbation radius and temporal horizon; treated prediction as one possible consequence of stabilization under delay.
5. Made internal physical worlds a central, anti-homuncular construct while marking the subject/world identity as a hypothesis.
6. Defined ACS as a provisional family of multiscale relational operators rather than a Pearson-correlation matrix; added ACS-space, metastability, quality-profile, scale, estimator, and causal limitations.
7. Preserved the EM manifestation claim while reformulating the CEMI contrast through mediation and residual field-level effects.
8. Expanded the identity, hard-problem, qualification, unity, language-game, long-spectrum, and structured-qualia discussions without claiming a solution.
9. Added theory comparisons with shared ground, precise divergence, separating evidence, and type of difference.
10. Added experimental programmes A–G and predictions P1–P7, each with paradigm, variables, measure, baseline, comparison, expected direction, disconfirmation, and theory specificity.
11. Kept AI subordinate and described PRAttention only as a possible engineering bridge.
12. Added twelve objections with a response and an explicit open problem.

## Remaining limitations

- ACS has no unique estimator or scale-equivalence criterion.
- The stabilization dynamics and local learning rules remain provisional.
- The framework has no validated empirical result and no consciousness score.
- The qualification and subject-individuation problems remain unresolved.
- Cross-subject relational convergence, field mediation, and minimal-system contrasts require dedicated preregistration and data.
- The generated LaTeX is a publication master but should be regenerated after conceptual Markdown edits to prevent drift.
