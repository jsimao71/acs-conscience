# v0.2 repository audit

Date: 2026-08-02

## Repository state and provenance

The repository was not clean at the start of the v0.2 work. Git reported the formerly tracked root manuscript files as deleted and the supplied `docs/` directory as untracked. The user identified `docs/embodied_conscience.md` and `docs/embodied_conscience.tex` as the working baselines, so the existing reorganization was preserved. No root milestone was restored or overwritten.

The supplied baseline artifacts were:

- `docs/embodied_conscience.md`: short conceptual outline, approximately 1,600 words;
- `docs/embodied_conscience.tex`: expanded v0.1 publication draft, approximately 5,200 words, with an inline bibliography;
- `docs/embodied_conscience.docx` and `docs/embodied_conscience.pdf`: generated baseline artifacts;
- `docs/AGENTS.md`: authoritative v0.2 requirements.

No prior reproducible build script, standalone BibTeX database, changelog, audit, completion report, or v0.2-named source existed.

## Companion material inspected

The mechanistic theory preview at `D:/git/rd/embodied_cognition` was inspected, especially its foundations, principles, glossary, mathematical draft, open questions, experimental roadmap, and literature anchors. The v0.2 paper selectively incorporates:

- continuity from physical self-organization through biological viability to cognition;
- self-perturbation and stabilization as complementary requirements;
- dynamic operation between silence, saturation, rigidity, and instability;
- abstraction as increasing stability over perturbation radius and temporal horizon;
- prediction as a possible consequence of stabilization under delay;
- the need to guard against representational collapse.

These ideas are presented as an early mechanistic research programme, not established results.

The PRAttention documentation at `D:/git/rd/pdattention/docs` was inspected only to delimit the subordinate AI section. PRAttention is described as an engineering bridge involving explicit references and progressive retrieval, not as a consciousness architecture.

## Baseline gaps relative to v0.2 requirements

The LaTeX baseline already had a cautious identity proposal, a theory-comparison table, a first ACS equation, five predictions, and a useful EM-versus-CEMI distinction. It nevertheless required substantial revision:

- action was stated too strongly as necessary for each stabilized percept;
- homeostasis, invariant pickup, and internal physical worlds were underdeveloped;
- ACS was operationalized mainly as a single correlation matrix rather than a multiscale family;
- candidate ACS dimensions and estimator limitations were absent;
- the field perturbation prediction unrealistically assumed unchanged generating ACS;
- the long-spectrum account collapsed distinct dimensions;
- the experimental programme and predictions lacked several required controls;
- the claim that restricting the ontology avoided combination “by construction” was too strong;
- objections, organoid caution, AI restraint, and format/build synchronization were incomplete.

## Source convention adopted

Markdown is the conceptual master. LaTeX is a generated publication master that supplies equations, numbered sections, citations, and PDF production. DOCX and PDF are generated artifacts. The convention and build commands are documented in the repository README.

## Citation policy

The bibliography preserves useful baseline sources and adds selectively verified primary studies, reviews, and scholarly books for homeostatic plasticity, allostasis/interoception, dynamic connectivity, metastability, representational similarity, perturbational complexity, consciousness-state dynamics, organoids, closed-loop cultures, multidimensional consciousness, and neighbouring theories. No experimental result is claimed for the present framework.
